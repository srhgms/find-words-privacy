Find Words Kids: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy is for parents and carers. Find Words Kids is a word-search game for children aged 3 to 12. It is published by srhgms, an independent developer based in Ukraine ("we", "us"). You can reach us at cyberaidev@protonmail.com.

The short version

Find Words Kids collects nothing about your child or about you. The game works without the internet. Everything it remembers stays on your device. There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no chat.

What the game keeps, and where

The game keeps a few things only on your device, so that it can carry on where your child left off:

  • Players: for each player (up to 4), an age band (3–4, 5–6, 7–9 or 10–12) and a picture chosen from the game's own set. No name, no birthday, no photo.
  • Progress: puzzles finished, words found and which words needed help, so that the game can bring them back for practice.
  • Settings: voice, volume, letters, motion, vibration, play-time limits you set, and the daily reminder if you turn it on.
  • Play time: minutes played on each of the last 14 days, so that your limits work and you can see them.
  • Today's free puzzles: how many of the day's 5 free puzzles have been used.
  • Subscription status: if you subscribe, whether the subscription is active and when it renews or ends, as your app store reported it. Never your payment details.

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. We cannot see it.

You can delete one player or all data at any time in the grown-ups area ("Delete all data"). Uninstalling the game also removes it.

No internet, no tracking

  • The game does not use the internet while your child plays. On Android it does not even ask for internet access.
  • There is no advertising of any kind, and no advertising identifier is used.
  • There are no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools from us or anyone else.
  • There are no accounts, no chat, no messages and no way for your child to share anything with other people.
  • The game does not use the camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Purchases

Find Words Kids can be played free, with 5 new puzzles a day. A grown-up can buy a subscription in the grown-ups area. The purchase is made with your app store (for example Google Play) under your store account. The store handles the payment and shares with the game only whether a subscription is active. Please read your app store's privacy policy to see what the store itself keeps about purchases.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder in the grown-ups area, your device shows one short message a day ("New puzzles are ready") at the time you choose. The reminder is set up on your device only. It is off unless you turn it on.

Links and support

Links to this policy or to support open only after the parental gate, and the game says when you are leaving it. If you email us for support, we use your email only to answer you.

Children's privacy laws

Because the game collects no personal information from children, it does not need to ask for parental consent to collect it. We designed it to meet the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, the EU and UK GDPR rules for children, the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, Ukrainian personal data law, and the Google Play Families and Apple Kids Category rules.

Changes

If we ever change what the game does with data, we will update this policy and the date above before the change reaches the game. We will not start collecting personal information from children.
