Find Words: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Find Words is a word-search game for players aged 13 and older. It is published by srhgms, an independent developer based in Ukraine ("we", "us"). You can reach us at cyberaidev@protonmail.com.

The short version

Today Find Words collects no personal information. You play without an account, and your progress stays on your device. There are no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking, now or later.

What the game keeps on your device

  • Puzzles in progress and finished, your level, looks, theme collections, Daily Challenge results and streak, and the rare words in your Lexicon.
  • Settings such as timer, score, sound, vibration and animations.
  • How many of today's free puzzles you have used.

This stays on your device and is not sent to us. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes it.

The website

When you play on our website, the web server receives your IP address in order to send you the game's files, as every website does. Server logs are kept short and minimal: we keep IP addresses only as long as needed for security, at most 7 days (FW-SEC-06). The website loads no third-party scripts, fonts or tracking pixels. (The hosting provider is chosen with the owner before launch and named here.)

No ads, no tracking

There are no ads of any kind, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

Purchases

A subscription removes the daily limit on new puzzles. In the app it is bought with your app store under your store account; the store handles payment. Subscribers never get an advantage in battles or on leaderboards.

When online play launches

These parts start only with accounts and online play, and this policy will be updated before then:

  • Age check: before an account is made we ask for month and year of birth. Under 13 (or the higher age your country sets for consent), no account is made and nothing is sent; you keep playing as a guest. We keep only that the check was passed, never the birth date.
  • Account data: email address, sign-in credential (passkey, password hash, or a token from Apple, Google or Microsoft), a generated display name, settings, progress, Lexicon, ratings and subscription status. No real name, photo, phone number, contacts or location.
  • Purpose: to sync your progress between devices, run battles and leaderboards fairly (scores and results are checked by our server, and scores are used to group players into skill bands and weekly tables; a result can be held for review), and to recognise your subscription on every platform.
  • Export and deletion: you can download all your data and delete your account in the game and on the website. Deletion removes it from live systems within 30 days and from backups within the backup period stated here.
  • Where data is stored: in the European Union.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU or UK GDPR, Ukrainian law or US state laws), you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, and you can complain to your data protection authority. Today we hold no personal data about players; contact us if you have a question.

Changes

We will update this policy and the date above before any change in what the game does with data.
